The Story of Arif Organic Farm

Arif Organic Farm began in 2011 with just five acres of exhausted land and a bold dream: to prove that agriculture can thrive without poisonous chemicals, artificial ripeners, or synthetic GMOs.

Through years of dedicated soil rehabilitation using organic cow manure, vermicomposting, and leguminous cover crops, we breathed life back into the microbial ecosystem. Today, our farm spans over 120 certified organic acres producing more than 45 seasonal vegetable and fruit crops.

We don't merely harvest crops; we nurture living ecosystems where pollinators, birds, and beneficial microorganisms protect our produce naturally.

✓ Zero synthetic pesticides, insecticides, or growth hormones.
✓ Annual third-party organic certification & soil toxicity tests.
✓ Direct-to-consumer model supporting fair livelihoods for local farm workers.
